Dota 2 Overlay - Twitch Extension

About

  • Webstroy Ltd.

  • 2018

Interactive video overlay extension for twitch streamers that presents the following information to their viewers:
  - Recent games played
  - In-depth details about past matches
  - Overall game performance
  - Heroes played and their success rate
  - List of records with filters

My role

Built solo, front-end to back-end. I developed the Vue.js overlay rendered over the stream, the PHP (Slim) API, and the service that pulls and processes players' Dota 2 match history - feeding the views for recent games, in-depth match breakdowns, overall performance, per-hero success rates, and a filterable records list. Packaging and Twitch extension review were mine too.

Technologies

  • HTML5
  • Vue.js
  • Webpack
  • PHP (Slim)

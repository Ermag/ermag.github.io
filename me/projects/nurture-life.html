Nurture Life

About

  • Nurture Life, Inc.

  • nurturelife.com

  • 2024

Nurture Life is a subscription meal-delivery service for babies, toddlers and kids - fresh, dietitian-designed meals and snacks that ship to the door and are ready in about a minute. The mobile app is where customers run their subscription: browsing and customising the weekly menu, swapping meals, adjusting quantities, and skipping or pausing weeks.

My role

A client project: I built the hybrid mobile app end to end with Ionic and Capacitor, working from their existing UI designs and adapting everything to their style guide - primarily engineering, with UX improvements folded in wherever there was room. I also owned the delivery side: the development workflow, the build process, and the release strategy across both app stores.

Vitalscry

About

  • Personal project

  • 2025 - Present

Vitalscry is a food-label scanner: point your camera at any label - no barcode needed - and it flags allergens, additives and questionable ingredients, then gives a clear verdict (Go For It, Caution, Avoid) tuned to the dietary concerns you care about. It reads labels in multiple languages and works on imported or brand-new products that barcode-based apps can't.

My role

A solo project, top to bottom - the app, the API, the design system and the mascot. The client is Vue 3 / Ionic / Capacitor (camera capture and on-device barcode scanning); the API is Node.js / Express, with label analysis running through a structured verdict model. That also meant owning the parts that aren't code: the "Paper Pastel" design language, the Rive mascot animation, RevenueCat subscriptions and scan-pack purchases, localisation across eight languages, and a fastlane pipeline that ships to both app stores.

Fixional

About

  • Fixional Inc.

  • 2019

Fixional is an editorial tool that evaluates clarity, originality, and completeness in writing.
Providing both a high-level overview as well as granular feedback, Fixional can be used by editors, publishers, and writers to augment their review and revision processes.

My role

The client provided rough mockups as a starting point; from there I owned the UI/UX end to end. I refined the flows into a complete, consistent interface and built it in React - including the two-tier feedback experience (a high-level overview alongside granular, inline suggestions), the views that surface Fixional's clarity, originality and completeness scoring, and the integration with the GraphQL API.

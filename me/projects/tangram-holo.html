An AI video-generation platform: you write a brief, pick a visual style and voice narrator, and it produces a finished narrated video - script, AI-generated visuals, animations, and voiceover - all composed and playable in the browser, ready to publish instantly. Built for creators, educators and marketers who need video without a production pipeline.

My role

I founded Tangram Holo and lead both product and engineering. It's a Vue 3 / Ionic client and a Node.js / Express API on MongoDB, Redis and Socket.IO. The demanding parts: an in-browser video engine, a generation backend that fans AI jobs out to various model providers and AI agent pipelines, and a credit-based billing system.
